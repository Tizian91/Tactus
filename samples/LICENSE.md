# Drum samples – licenses

The recordings in this folder are **not** under the license of Tactus (see `../LICENSE.md`).
They come from two free libraries. Changes made for Tactus: three strengths per sound chosen,
mixed to mono, resampled to 32 kHz, decay shortened and faded out, levels evened
(`tools/build_samples.sh`).
